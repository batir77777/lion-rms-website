REGULATORY LANDSCAPE

CQC Inspectors Will Ask. We Make Sure You Have the Answers.

CQC's Key Lines of Enquiry (KLOEs) under the Safe domain require documented, up-to-date fire safety and H&S evidence. "It's all in a folder somewhere" doesn't pass inspection. Our digital platform means your evidence is organised, searchable, and ready the moment the inspector arrives.

We cover every piece of legislation the sector touches — from the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 to the Health and Social Care Act 2008.

Get CQC-Ready →
RR(FS)O 2005

Fire risk assessment mandatory for all care premises. Must be reviewed after any significant change.

L8 ACoP

Legionella risk assessment and written control scheme required for all healthcare water systems.

HSWA 1974

Written H&S policy and risk assessments required for all employers. CQC uses these as evidence.

HSC Act 2008

Regulation 12 (safe care) requires evidence of robust systems for identifying and managing risk.

CDM 2015

Applies to any refurbishment or building work in care settings — often overlooked until it's too late.

COSHH 2002

Control of Substances Hazardous to Health — chemical risk assessments for cleaning products, medications handling areas.
