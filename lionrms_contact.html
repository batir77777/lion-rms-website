Common Questions

Quick Answers

How quickly can you carry out an assessment?

For most premises we can be on-site within 5–10 working days. Urgent compliance cases (e.g. enforcement action, insurance deadlines) are prioritised — contact us directly.

Do you cover the whole of the UK?

Yes. We cover all of the UK for on-site assessments and training. Digital and AI services are delivered remotely and available worldwide.

How much does a fire risk assessment cost?

Pricing depends on the size and complexity of your premises. Most standard commercial FRAs start from £250. Contact us for a fixed-price quote — no hidden extras.

Can you help if we're already non-compliant?

Absolutely. That's often when we're most useful. We'll carry out the assessment, issue the report, and help you close the action plan — quickly and defensibly.

What if I only need help with admin, not compliance?

That's fine. Our AI admin and digital services are completely standalone. You don't need to be a compliance client to benefit from process automation.

Can you work with our existing H&S management system?

Yes. We integrate with most existing systems or build a fresh workflow around your current tools. We don't force you to switch to something new unless it genuinely helps.
