# Lion RMS — Compliance Platform Demo
### Script, shot list & on-screen captions for a 45–60s website advert

---

## ⚠️ Before you record — anonymise everything

Your platform holds real client fire-safety data. **Do not film live records.** Either:
- Record from a **demo account** with obviously fake data — e.g. *"Demo University", "Sample Court (Managing Agent)", "Riverside Construction Site", "1 Example Street, London E1"*, invented findings and dates; or
- Blur client names, addresses, photos and any identifiable findings in editing.

A clean demo account looks more professional on camera than blurred redactions. Use it.

**Quick checklist before hitting record:** no real client names · no real addresses/postcodes · no real site photos · no email addresses · no phone numbers · browser bookmarks bar hidden · notifications off (Windows Focus Assist on).

---

## Recording setup (Windows, free)

- **Tool:** ScreenPal or Loom (screen + voice, easiest), or Clipchamp (built into Windows 11), or OBS Studio (most control).
- **Resolution:** record at 1920×1080. Maximise the browser; hide the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B).
- **Cursor:** move **slowly and deliberately**; pause on each screen for ~2 seconds before clicking. Jerky, fast navigation reads as amateur.
- **Length:** keep raw clips short; you'll trim to 45–60s total.
- **Two versions to export:** (1) with your voiceover, (2) muted with on-screen captions — most people watch web video on mute, so the caption version is the one that autoplays on the site.
