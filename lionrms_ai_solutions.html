What People Ask Before Starting

Do we need to change our existing software?

Usually not. We build around the tools you already have — Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, your CRM. We only recommend new tools when there's a genuine gap that your current stack can't fill, and we never push tool subscriptions we don't fully believe in.

We've already tried AI tools. Why would this be different?

Because we implement AI into your specific workflows, rather than showing you a general tool and leaving you to figure it out. Most failed AI adoption comes from teams using generic prompts for professional work. We build custom prompts, proper processes, and accountability frameworks around your actual work.

How does remote delivery work if you need to understand our processes?

All our engagements are remote-first by design. The workflow audit is done via video call with a structured framework — we've found it's as effective as being on-site, and it means we can work with clients in any country without travel costs. We've delivered for clients in the USA, UAE, Germany, and across the UK entirely remotely.

What sectors have you worked in?

The methodology started in fire safety and H&S consultancy — one of the most process-intensive sectors there is. We've since applied it to law firms, healthcare, construction, manufacturing, property management, professional services, and more. The workflow audit reveals what to automate regardless of sector.

What's the minimum size of team or organisation?

There's no minimum. We've worked with solo practitioners (1 person) and organisations with large teams across multiple sites. For a solo consultant, even 5–6 hours a week of admin time saved has a significant revenue impact. For larger teams, the multiplier effect is substantial.

What does the free workflow audit involve?

A 60–90 minute remote session where we map your key processes, identify where time is going, and give you an honest picture of what AI can and can't do for your specific situation. You get a written summary with 3–5 prioritised opportunities and time estimates — whether or not you choose to work with us.
