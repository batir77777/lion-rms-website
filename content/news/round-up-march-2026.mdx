A record of March 2026. This page is not rewritten as events move on — later
developments are reported in later items, so this stays a picture of what was
known at the time.

## Approved Document B review opens

On 25 March the Building Safety Regulator and MHCLG opened a consultation on
changes to Approved Document B, covering external wall and balcony guidance,
thresholds on combustible structural elements, and a recommendation for evacuation
lifts in residential buildings above 18 metres. It runs to 1 July.

## BS 8214:2026 published

BSI published BS 8214:2026 on 20 March, superseding the 2016 edition and bringing
smoke control doors explicitly into the title and scope of the fire door code of
practice.

<NewsLink slug="bs-8214-2026-fire-door-standard-published">Full item, and what it means for inspection work</NewsLink>.

## Asbestos prosecution at Cannock

Birmingham Magistrates' Court sentenced two companies and a site manager on
6 March following a large-scale asbestos disturbance at a demolition site where a
survey had already identified asbestos-containing materials. Fines of £74,900 and
£13,400, a 26-week suspended sentence and a five-year director disqualification.

## What we took from the month

Two of these three are about currency rather than substance. A new consultation
and a new edition of a code of practice both create the same practical risk: work
carried out against a reference that has since moved, by someone who did not
notice it had. The prosecution is the reminder of what the other end of that
looks like.
