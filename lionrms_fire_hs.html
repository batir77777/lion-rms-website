Legal Framework

What the Law Requires

Ignorance is not a defence. Here is what UK law actually mandates for your business.

Fire Safety

Every non-domestic premises must have a suitable and sufficient fire risk assessment carried out by a "competent person" — and kept up to date.

RR(FS)O 2005

Health & Safety

Employers with five or more employees must have a written health and safety policy and carry out suitable risk assessments for all significant hazards.

HSWA 1974 / MHSWR 1999

Legionella / Water

Duty holders must identify and manage Legionella risks in water systems. This requires a written risk assessment and a documented control scheme.

L8 ACoP / HSG 274

Construction (CDM)

Projects with more than one contractor require a Principal Designer and a written Construction Phase Plan before work begins on site.

CDM Regulations 2015

Environmental

Businesses that produce, handle or dispose of waste have legal duties of care. Certain activities require permits under the Environmental Permitting Regulations.

Environment Act 2021 / EPR

Enforcement

The HSE and local fire authorities carry out unannounced inspections. Non-compliance can result in prohibition notices, improvement notices, prosecution and unlimited fines.

Enforcement Action
