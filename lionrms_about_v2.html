The System Was Built in
Fire Safety

The story of Lion RMS starts not with AI, but with a problem familiar to every compliance professional: too much time on paperwork, too little time on the actual work.

As a fire risk assessor and health & safety consultant, I was spending six hours writing a risk assessment report that took one hour to conduct. Policies, procedures, inductions, audit records, review schedules — all of it generated manually, from scratch, every time, for every client.

The knowledge in my head was professional and specialist. The time I was spending getting it into documents was not. So I started implementing AI tools — systematically, rigorously, tested against the standards that fire safety requires. Not "ChatGPT in a browser tab," but properly embedded into the workflow: structured inputs, trained prompts, quality control frameworks, professional outputs.

"A fire risk assessment that took 6 hours to write now takes 35 minutes. The quality is the same. The content is better — because the assessor is focused on findings, not formatting."

That system — audit the workflow, design the AI-assisted version, implement it, measure the results — worked. Consistently. The time savings were real, measurable, and defensible under regulatory scrutiny.

Then the Same Pattern Appeared Everywhere

The conversations with clients in other sectors started revealing the same picture. A law firm where associates spent 40% of their week on document preparation. A manufacturer where the quality team was writing up audit findings in six-hour report sessions. A healthcare provider where CQC evidence packs were assembled manually every inspection cycle. A property group where landlord compliance documentation lived in a mix of email, spreadsheets, and memory.

Different sectors. Different regulatory frameworks. Same fundamental problem: skilled professionals spending most of their week on structured, repetitive document and record work that AI could handle in minutes.

The solution was the same too. Map the workflows. Identify what AI can replace. Build the automations. Train the team. Measure the results at Day 30.

What Lion RMS Is Today

Lion RMS is a global AI workflow automation consultancy — working with companies in the USA, UK, Europe, and the Middle East to implement AI into their daily operations. The fire & H&S practice continues as a core service: it's where the methodology was built and proven, and it remains an area of deep specialist expertise.

Delivered entirely remotely. Results measured at Day 30. Accountability built in because the system was built in an environment where accountability was non-negotiable.

That's the foundation. Process rigour from fire safety. Applied at scale, globally, through AI.
