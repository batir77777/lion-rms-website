WHY IT MATTERS

The Consequences of Getting It Wrong in Hospitality Are Public

Unlike an office H&S failure, hospitality incidents involve guests, media coverage, and licence reviews. The stakes are higher — and regulators know it.

🔥

Fire — Immediate Closure Risk

The Fire and Rescue Authority can serve an Alterations Notice or Prohibition Notice that closes your venue immediately. No appeal window. No notice period. The FRA doesn't ask if it's your busiest Saturday.

Penalty: unlimited fine + immediate closure + prosecution
💧

Legionella — Reputational Catastrophe

A hotel guest contracting Legionnaires' disease makes national news. An inadequate Legionella risk assessment or a gap in your monitoring records means you face both prosecution and a media storm simultaneously.

Penalty: unlimited fine + civil liability + media coverage
🍺

Licence Review — Premises Licence at Risk

Local authorities can review your Premises Licence if there are "problems associated with the premises." Fire safety failures and H&S enforcement notices are grounds for review. Your licence is your business.

Penalty: licence suspension, additional conditions, or revocation
📋

EHO Inspection — No Warning Given

Environmental Health Officers can inspect unannounced. They check H&S alongside food hygiene. A fire risk assessment in a binder with no evidence of annual review is a fixed penalty notice waiting to happen.

Penalty: £300–£30,000 fixed penalty notices
🏨

Multi-Site — One Weak Link

If you operate multiple venues, one site's poor compliance can trigger a group-wide inspection. A Prohibition Notice at one property affects investor confidence and group insurance rates across your estate.

Penalty: group-wide enforcement + insurance premium increases
📰

TripAdvisor & Online Reputation

An evacuation, a guest complaint about fire alarm systems, or a news story about an enforcement notice will appear in reviews and search results alongside your booking page. Compliance is brand protection.

Impact: direct revenue loss from booking decline
